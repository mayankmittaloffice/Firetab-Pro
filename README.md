# Firetab-Pro
Firetab Pro replaces your New Tab page with a calm, fast start page that looks different every day.
	
	A NEW LOOK EVERY DAY
	• 10 color themes: 5 light (Paper, Stone, Blush, Mist, Lilac) and 5 dark (Midnight, Forest, Dusk, Sea, Graphite)
	• 6 generative art styles drawn behind the page: Orbits, Waves, Contours, Constellation, Fan and Weave
	• The look changes once a day at local midnight, and the same theme never appears two days in a row
	• Light, Dark or Auto mode (Auto follows your device)
	• Want something different? Shuffle for a new look, or pin the one you love and keep it
	
	SEARCH YOUR WAY
	Choose Perplexity, Copilot, Brave, Google, YouTube or DuckDuckGo with one click and search straight from the new tab.
	
	YOUR SHORTCUTS
	Add, edit and remove shortcut tiles for the sites you visit most, with a built-in icon picker. It starts with Instagram, YouTube, Facebook, Twitter, Email and WhatsApp, and you can change all of them.
	
	CLOCK AND DATE
	A live clock with the date and weekday, right on the page.
	
	PRIVATE BY DESIGN
	No account, no analytics, no tracking, no ads. Your shortcuts and look settings are stored only on your device. When you search, your query goes directly to the search engine you picked. Firetab Pro never sees it.
