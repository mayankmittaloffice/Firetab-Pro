Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Firetab Pro ("the extension") replaces your browser's New Tab page. This policy explains what it does with your information.

What We Collect

Nothing. The extension has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no servers. The developer does not receive any information from you.

What Is Stored on Your Device

The extension saves your shortcuts (name, link and icon), your Auto/Light/Dark choice and, if you pin a look, that look. This is kept in your browser's local storage on your own device.

It is never sent to the developer or to anyone else, and it is removed when you uninstall the extension or clear its data.

Searching

When you type in the search box and press Enter, your browser goes to the search engine you selected (Perplexity, Microsoft Copilot or Bing, Brave Search, Google, YouTube or DuckDuckGo) with your query.

That service handles your query under its own privacy policy. Firetab Pro does not see or store your searches.

Shortcuts

Clicking a shortcut tile takes you to the website you saved. Firetab Pro does not track which shortcuts you use.

Third-Party Code

Firetab Pro does not load remote code. Fonts and icons are bundled inside the extension.

Permissions

The extension requests no special browser permissions.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version and date will appear on this page.
